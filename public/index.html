Jarvis is free. Out of the box he uses a free "starter" AI that can only answer about once every 30 seconds. A free Groq key makes him answer in about 1 second. No credit card, no subscription, about 2 minutes:


  1. Opens console.groq.com/keys in your browser.
  2. 2. Sign in. The easiest is "Continue with Google", or use your email.
  3. 3. Click "Create API Key", type the name Jarvis, press Submit, then press Copy.
  4. 4. Paste it here and press Save:

The key is saved encrypted on this computer only. Want even more speed and no daily limits? Add more free keys (Cerebras, Gemini, Mistral) in API keys, or set up the unlimited offline AI under AI brain.

The wake word follows the name ("Hey Friday"). It works best with a common English word or name.

Each voice downloads once, then works offline. Try a few with "Test voice". "Charles" and "Henry" are the closest to a refined AI butler; "Norman" and "John" sound like older gentlemen. Voices that copy real actors aren't included.

Closest match: in ElevenLabs open Voices → Add voice → Voice Design and describe it, e.g. "Refined, calm, middle-aged British male AI butler, RP accent, crisp and precise, dry understated wit, slightly digital polish." Save it, then paste its Voice ID below. For an older butler try "elderly, warm, slightly gravelly London gentleman butler, unhurried and kind". Describe a style, don't copy a real actor.

Best for accents: with a free Groq key (or an OpenAI key), Jarvis hears you with Whisper, which understands accents from all over the world. Without a key it uses the free offline engine; other languages download their own offline model once. Jarvis answers in the language you speak.

On phones, Jarvis uses the phone's own speech recognition when it can. Tip: on Windows, add the "English (United Kingdom)" speech pack in Windows Settings → Time & language → Speech for a British built-in voice.
